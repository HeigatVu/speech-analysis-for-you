# Findings: Vietnamese `.cha` quality, new ASR arms and alignment (2026-10-04)

Sessions: pilot p001 and p002 only (channel 0, approved SHA-256, hash checked before decode).
p003 to p005 are not scored: they have no reference transcript and no approved digest.
Host: RTX 5070 12 GB, `--device cuda`, shared with ollama's 4.4 GB embedding model.
No transcript text, audio or absolute paths appear here.

## 1. What changed in `main`

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Qwen3-ASR segments span their decode window | They had no timing, so no bullets, no `%wor`, and every utterance was labelled PAR |
| `run --align wav2vec2-vi` (reuses `alignment.py`) | Times the words of word-less segments; unalignable words stay untimed |
| Qwen3 numerals and `%` spoken as words | `70` and `%` made chatter reject the file (E220, E316) |
| New ASR arms `whisper-large-v3`, `wav2vec2-vi`, `zipformer-vi` | Requested model comparison |
| Benchmark records revision and device; `--asr-models` | Closes the known provenance gap |
| Qwen3 loads in fp16 at load time | 4.70 GB to 4.08 GB peak at load |

## 5. Negative results and caveats

- **wav2vec2-vi as ASR:** the pilot audio is very quiet (peak 0.044, RMS 0.0019). Greedy CTC output
  had almost no word breaks (5 "words" in 317 characters on one 12 s window) and 0.03 character
  similarity to Qwen3. It stays useful as the aligner. It was added as an arm and is not recommended.
- **whisper-large-v3:** worse than the Vietnamese-tuned models even with language pinned; no
  hallucination analysis was done.
- **Alignment accuracy is unverified:** 96 to 97% of words get a span, but no human-timed reference
  exists, so span correctness is not measured. Utterance bullets from Qwen3 and Zipformer are
  tightened to the first and last aligned word only when every lexical word was timed.
- **Zipformer** reports syllable start times but no ends, so ends were not invented.
- **Not built:** ROVER, ViBERT punctuation restoration, hotwords, Conv-TasNet, Vietnamese utterance
  segmentation (talkbank-tools has no Vietnamese model). The Qwen3 forced aligner does not support
  Vietnamese (upstream map covers yue, zho, cmn, eng only).
- Provenance: PhoWhisper and Qwen3 cells show no revision because they ran unpinned from the local
  cache. Zipformer and wav2vec2 are pinned.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Use `--asr-model qwen3-asr --align wav2vec2-vi` for drafts: best accuracy, valid CHAT, ~48 s per
   17 min session on GPU.
2. Use `zipformer-vi` when the GPU is busy (about 34 s on CPU, 0.307 WER).
3. Keep PhoWhisper for native word timing, and run it alone on the card.
4. Next measurement, not a build: a human-timed excerpt to score alignment error.
